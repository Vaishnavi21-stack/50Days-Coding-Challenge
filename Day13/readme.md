# 📊 Day 13 – Stress Level Analysis (Power BI Dashboard)

## 🔍 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee stress levels using Power BI.
The goal is to identify patterns, understand contributing factors, and provide insights to improve employee well-being and productivity.

---

## 📁 Dataset Description

The dataset includes the following fields:

* EmployeeID
* Department
* Age
* Gender
* WorkHours
* SleepHours
* ExerciseHours
* CaffeineIntake
* StressLevel (1 = Low, 5 = High)
* DateRecorded

---

## 📊 KPIs Created

* Average Stress Level
* Percentage of Employees with High Stress (StressLevel ≥ 4)
* Average Sleep Hours
* Average Work Hours

---

## 📈 Visualizations

* Column Chart: Average Stress Level by Department
* Line Chart: Stress Level Trend Over Time
* Scatter Plot: Work Hours vs Stress Level (with trend analysis)
* Donut Chart: Stress Level Distribution (Low, Medium, High)
* Table: Highlighted high-risk employees using conditional formatting

---

## 🎛️ Filters (Slicers)

* Department
* Gender
* Age Group

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

* Employees working longer hours tend to have higher stress levels.
* Lower sleep hours are associated with higher stress levels.
* Certain departments show consistently higher average stress.
* A significant portion of employees fall under medium to high stress categories.
* Stress levels fluctuate over time with noticeable peaks.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* Power BI
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

---

## 📌 Conclusion

This dashboard helps in identifying stress patterns and high-risk employees, enabling organizations to take proactive steps toward improving employee well-being.
